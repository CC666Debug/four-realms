# Four Realms

Four radio apps in one: **🎃 Halloween**, **🌆 Synth**, **🌙 Pagan** and **⛧ Kvlt**. Each realm keeps its own look, stations and extras, and one player keeps the music going while you move between them. Listen on your phone, or cast to Google Nest speakers and speaker groups.

**▶ Open the app: https://cc666debug.github.io/four-realms/**

The four realms are also their own apps: [Halloween Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/halloween-caster/), [Synth Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/synth-caster/), [Pagan Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/pagan-caster/) and [Kvlt Kaster](https://cc666debug.github.io/kvlt-kaster/).

## Moving between realms

- **Title screen.** Four doors greet you each time the app opens.
- **Tabs** along the bottom: one tap to any realm. A small dot marks the realm that's playing.
- **Swipe** sideways to flip to the next realm.
- **Portal.** Tap the realm's name at the top for a ring of four doors.

## The realms

- **🎃 Halloween:** every free Halloween Radio channel first, then 20 more Halloween stations (horror, psychobilly, old-time radio and more), with a countdown to Halloween. Halloween Radio's channels show the song's cover art, with links to **👻 Bewitch on site** and **🦴 Spine-Tingler**. **📶 Data saver** plays their 64 kbps version on your phone (it also turns on by itself on a weak signal). Supporters can add **⭐ Premium**: the link stays on your phone only, and it's shared with Halloween Caster on the same phone.
- **🌆 Synth:** hundreds of synthwave, darkwave, goth, EBM, synth-pop, new wave and chiptune stations.
- **🌙 Pagan:** pagan, Wiccan, Norse, Celtic and medieval folk, with the moon phase and the next sabbat.
- **⛧ Kvlt:** black metal and its neighbours, with the moon phase and the next unholy date.

## What it does

- **Search, genre filters and sorting** in each realm; each realm remembers its own.
- **Shuffle**, next and previous stay in the realm that's playing, even while you browse another.
- **Song names** for stations marked ♪, on screen and on your lock screen.
- **Favorites.** Tap ☆ on any station.
- **Sleep timer.** Long-press play/stop to pick 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes. The music fades out over the last minute.
- **Cast to speakers** with the cast button (Chrome on Android or a computer).
- **Keeps playing through drop-outs**, and opens instantly, even offline, once added to your home screen.

## Privacy

No accounts, no tracking. Favorites and settings stay on your device.

Streams belong to their stations. This is a free fan project.
